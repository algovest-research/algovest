<h3>Key Revenue Insights:</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Consistent double-digit revenue growth</strong> over the past 5 years, with FY2026 showing 11.2% growth to ₹1,30,144 Cr — a new all-time high.</li>
<li><strong>Margins are under pressure:</strong> Operating Profit Margin (OPM) has declined from a peak of 27% (FY2021) to 21% (FY2026). This is the lowest OPM in the last 6 fiscal years.</li>
<li><strong>Net Profit declined 4.3% in FY2026</strong> despite revenue growing 11.2% — a significant red flag. This was driven by:<ul>
<li>Lower Other Income (₹574 Cr vs ₹2,485 Cr in FY2025 — a massive drop)</li>
<li>Higher Interest costs (₹869 Cr vs ₹644 Cr)</li>
<li>Higher Depreciation (₹4,355 Cr vs ₹4,084 Cr)</li>
<li>Stable tax rate at ~25%</li>
</ul>
</li>

<h2>8. Key Risks &amp; Negative Factors</h2>
<ol>
<li><span style="color:#dc2626;font-weight:600">✗</span> <strong>Margin Compression</strong> — OPM declining from 27% (FY2021) to 21% (FY2026) is concerning. Rising employee costs, visa costs, and competitive pricing pressure in the IT services industry.</li>
<li><span style="color:#dc2626;font-weight:600">✗</span> <strong>Net Profit Decline in FY2026</strong> — Despite 11.2% revenue growth, net profit fell 4.3% — indicating cost pressures and lower other income.</li>
<li><span style="color:#dc2626;font-weight:600">✗</span> <strong>Stock Near 52-Week Low</strong> — The price of ₹1,164 is just 5.5% above the 52-week low of ₹1,103 and 35% below the 52-week high of ₹1,780 — bearish momentum.</li>
<li><span style="color:#dc2626;font-weight:600">✗</span> <strong>Declining Operating Cash Flow</strong> — CFO dropped from ₹22,448 Cr (FY2024) to ₹19,975 Cr (FY2026), a ~11% decline.</li>
<li><span style="color:#dc2626;font-weight:600">✗</span> <strong>High Dividend Payout (88%)</strong> — While good for income, such high payouts limit retained earnings for growth investments.</li>
<li><span style="color:#dc2626;font-weight:600">✗</span> <strong>Other Income Volatility</strong> — FY2026 saw other income drop to ₹574 Cr from ₹2,485 Cr in FY2025, significantly impacting bottom line.</li>
<li><span style="color:#dc2626;font-weight:600">✗</span> <strong>Rising Other Liabilities</strong> — Surged to ₹35,878 Cr (up 26% YoY), potentially indicating higher payables or provisions.</li>
</ol>
